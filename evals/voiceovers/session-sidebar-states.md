# session-sidebar-states — Distinct, readable session states

1. The sessions sidebar is visually distinct again, with a contained panel and clear separation from the main workspace.

2. The selected session has its own subtle background, while every title and timestamp stays aligned and easy to scan.

3. When a session is loading, a compact spinner uses reserved space beside the title, so it never overlaps or pushes text outside the row.

4. A newly updated unread session stands out with stronger text and a small unread indicator without competing with the selected state.

5. After I open the unread session, its unread indicator clears and the normal session styling returns.
