# signin-redesign — The first screen is honest: one headline, one primary action, nothing fake

Screen 03 of the Cloud Welcome Paper page (onboarding rethink, Jul 21–22).
Copy, hierarchy, and honesty fixes on the desktop welcome screen; zero
behavior change. The headline appeared twice (left subtitle + right panel
title), capability cards borrowed brand glyphs (Apple/Zapier/Medium/Stripe)
implying fake integrations, the Cloud card's subtitle was truncated, and the
on-premises/Daytona plumbing leaked into the product surface.

1. I open OpenWork for the first time and get one calm screen: the OpenWork mark, one headline — "Your computer, but it works for you." — three numbered steps, and a single primary button.

2. The right panel no longer repeats the headline — a quiet label, "WHAT OPENWORK CAN DO", sits above plain-text capability cards, with no borrowed brand logos pretending to be integrations.

3. Under the primary button, an "or" divider leads to two equal cards — "Use OpenWork Cloud" and "Join your organization" — each with a full sentence underneath, nothing cut off mid-thought.

4. There's nothing on this screen that isn't for me — the on-premises and folder-path plumbing is gone from the normal first run.

5. Developers still get their tools: in dev mode the same screen shows that plumbing under an explicit "Developer" label, clearly separated from the product.
